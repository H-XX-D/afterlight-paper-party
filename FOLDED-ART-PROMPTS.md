# Folded scenery and physical challenge artwork

These assets were generated with the built-in image creator in this task. The existing original `public/assets/world.webp` was the visual reference. The briefs below record the composition and style constraints used for this asset set; they are normalized production briefs, not a claim to be verbatim tool transcripts. Runtime WebP assets are committed and embedded in the portable HTML. Raw generated PNG masters remain local outside the repository.

**Shared direction:** original gothic paper theater, inked charcoal and ivory, restrained sage, copper, frost and violet pigments; illustrated cardstock grain, visible creases, folded side faces, cut rims and tiny worn foil details. Match the existing world illustration. No Nintendo characters, game logos, watermarks, legible UI text or generic geometric substitute artwork. Scenery should have a readable central play area, separated near and far architecture, and room for original cutout travelers.

## Clean outline corrective pass

These four built-in edits preserve the source atlas layouts and transparent openings. Smooth charcoal outlines, ivory cardstock, restrained sage/brass/violet pigments, crease shading and complete silhouettes are the shared brief. Pixel art, plastic CGI, new scenery, UI text and added objects were excluded. Saturated generation-marker fringes persisted in portions of the returned alpha gutters; production sprite preparation removes those markers once during cache construction, rather than drawing them into the game.
